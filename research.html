Publications

  1. VIX Option Pricing through Nonaffine GARCH Dynamics and Semi-analytical Formula

    With Qi Wang and Yuanyuan Zhang

    Journal of Futures Markets, 44, 1189–1223, 2024. Journal article

    Develops semi-analytical VIX option pricing approximations under nonaffine GARCH dynamics and compares their performance across empirically relevant volatility models.

Working Papers

  1. Do Sobolev Density Expansions Improve Option Pricing?

    With Qi Wang and Lu Zhang

    Under review at Mathematical Finance

    Constructs density approximation methods in Sobolev space and applies them to VIX density approximation and VIX option pricing under continuous-time models.

  2. Mutual Fund Outflows, Dealer Networks, and Corporate Bond Risk Premia

    Solo-authored · First-year paper, Mays Business School

    Scheduled presentation: EWMES 2026

    Develops a continuous-time OTC model of corporate bond markets with forced liquidation by bond funds and maturity-dependent concentration costs for funds and dealers. The model studies how intermediary frictions shape price impact, dealer-to-fund pricing, and the term structure of corporate bond yields during liquidity stress.

  3. Whose Beliefs Price Risk? Portfolio Constraints and the Marginal Investor

    Solo-authored · Second-year paper, Mays Business School

    Develops a continuous-time equilibrium model in which collateral and short-sale constraints attenuate the transmission of heterogeneous beliefs into portfolios and shift marginal pricing influence. Links weak belief-to-portfolio pass-through to risk premia and, under Gaussian beliefs, shows that the slope relative to its frictionless benchmark identifies the share of locally unconstrained investors.
