My name is Junting (pronounced approximately june-ting). I am a third-year Ph.D. student in Finance at Mays Business School, Texas A&M University. My research interests include asset pricing, financial intermediation, financial econometrics, and derivatives.

My current research studies how intermediary frictions, portfolio constraints, and heterogeneous beliefs shape asset prices. I also work on volatility modeling and option pricing, building on interests developed earlier in my academic career.

Before joining Texas A&M, I earned two bachelor's degrees: a B.A. in Finance and a B.S. in Mathematics from Southwestern University of Finance and Economics.
